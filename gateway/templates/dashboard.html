Why server-rendered rather than a JS app (DECISIONS.md): keeps v1 minimal, and keeps the conceptual separation visible — Grafana is the infrastructure dashboard, this is the product dashboard, and they should look and be built like different things rather than two tabs of the same tool. #} Product Usage — Inference Gateway
